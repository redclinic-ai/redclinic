Pricing questions

What is the pilot?
The first {{site.pricing.pilotSeats}} clinics to join get RedClinic at Rs {{site.pricing.pilotDayPrice}} a day, half the Clinic price. The founding team sets each pilot clinic up personally, and pilot clinics' feedback decides what we build next. The exact pilot terms, including how long the price is held, are confirmed in writing before you sign.
Is RedClinic ready to use now?
RedClinic is being built now and pilot clinics are set up first, as each part is ready. Your demo shows exactly what works today. You don't pay for anything until we've agreed your pack in writing.
Is GST included?
No. GST is added to every price on this page at the applicable rate.
Which billing period should I choose?
Monthly is the simplest way to start. Six-month packs are paid in one go for 180 days. Yearly packs give you 12 months for the price of 11.
What if I use up my minutes?
The app shows your minutes as you use them. If you regularly need more, we'll move you to a pack sized to your call volume. We'll agree any extra-minute rate with you before it applies.
Is WhatsApp included?
WhatsApp messaging isn't in the first version. When it arrives we'll tell you what it includes and costs before anything changes on your bill.
How do I pay?
Not on this website. After your demo we send the pack details and an invoice for the period you choose.
