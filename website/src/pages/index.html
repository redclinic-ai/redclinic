What changes when she joins your clinic.

  • Every call gets answered.

    Busy, at lunch or closed for the day, she picks up, helps the patient and books them in. Anyone you missed gets a call back.

  • Fewer empty chairs.

    She calls patients before their appointment. If someone doesn't turn up, she calls to find them a new time.

  • Patients keep coming back.

    She reminds them when their next sitting, adjustment or check-up is due, all year round, so treatments get finished.

  • Your whole clinic in one place.

    Appointments, patient records, X-rays, prescriptions and every call, in one app. Ask her and she finds it.
