Good clinics still lose patients in the gaps between appointments.

Not because of the dentistry. Because nobody had a free moment at the right time.

The phone rings mid-procedure

Your receptionist is billing a patient at the desk. The caller doesn't leave a message. They try the next clinic on Google.

Second sittings don't get booked

No-shows go quiet

Someone misses their slot. Calling them back to rebook is on the list, but the day fills up first.

RedClinic answers, follows up and remembers, so your team doesn't have to chase.

AnswersNEPA picks up when your team can't.

NEPA is the AI receptionist inside RedClinic. It answers patient calls on your clinic number in English, Hindi or Kannada, using the answers and timings you've approved.

  • Tells callers it's an AI assistant and passes them to your staff if they'd rather talk to a person.
  • Recognises existing patients and asks new callers what they need.
  • Requests a slot from your real calendar, ready for staff to approve.
  • Hands urgent calls to staff at once: pain, swelling, bleeding or a complaint.

RemembersOne record for every patient, from the first call.

Each patient gets a permanent RedClinic ID. Visits, calls, transcripts, prescriptions and reports your staff upload all stay together, even if their phone number changes.

  • Doctors can ask NEPA about a patient's history and get an answer linked to the original record.
  • NEPA doesn't diagnose or prescribe. It finds what's already on file.

Questions dentists ask us

Will patients know they're talking to an AI?
Yes. NEPA says it's the clinic's AI assistant at the start of every call. If a caller would rather speak to a person, NEPA passes the call to your staff.
What if a patient is in pain or it's an emergency?
NEPA never gives medical advice. When a caller mentions pain, swelling, bleeding or an injury, NEPA hands the call to your staff straight away and marks it urgent. If nobody picks up, it goes to the top of Needs attention.
Does NEPA book appointments by itself?
Not unless you allow it. By default NEPA requests the slot and a staff member approves it with one tap. The clinic owner can switch on automatic booking for specific workflows later.
Which languages does NEPA speak?
English, Hindi and Kannada to begin with, including calls that mix Hindi and English. More Indian languages are planned.
Do I need a new phone number?
No. You forward calls from your existing clinic number to NEPA. We'll guide your team through the setting, and you can switch it off at any time.
Can I bring in my existing patients?
Yes. During setup we import your patient list from an Excel or CSV file, so NEPA recognises returning patients from the first day.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. RedClinic runs in the browser on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to install.
How much does it cost?
RedClinic is a monthly subscription per clinic, with plans based on how many call minutes you need. We'll go through the plans and any one-time setup fee in your demo, before you decide anything.
Who can see my patients' records?
Only your clinic's staff, with the permissions you give them. Records are kept separate for each clinic. RedClinic's own team can access clinic data to run and support the service, and that access is logged. More on our security page.
