Questions clinic owners ask us

What is NEPA?
NEPA, short for Network of Expert Practice Agents, is the AI brain of RedClinic. Each clinic gets its own NEPA, personalised to its doctors, services and patients. You talk to it or type to it to find information and get work done, and it handles patient calls and follow-ups.
Can we give it our own name?
Yes. You name your AI when you sign up, for example Priya or Ruhi. Your team and your patients see and hear that name everywhere. NEPA is the technology underneath.
Will patients know they're talking to an AI?
Yes. She introduces herself by name as the clinic's AI assistant at the start of every call. If a caller would rather speak to a person, she passes the call to your staff.
What if a patient is in pain or it's an emergency?
NEPA never gives medical advice. When a caller mentions pain, bleeding, swelling or anything urgent, NEPA hands the call to your staff straight away and marks it urgent. If nobody picks up, it goes to the top of Needs attention.
Which clinics is RedClinic for?
Small and medium clinics of any speciality, from a single doctor to several branches. Dental follow-ups are ready first, skin is next, and pilot clinics help decide the order after that. Diagnostic centres are on the list too.
Does NEPA book appointments by itself?
Not unless you allow it. By default NEPA requests the slot and a staff member approves it with one tap. The clinic owner can switch on automatic booking for specific workflows later.
Which languages does NEPA speak?
English, Hindi and Kannada to begin with, including calls that mix Hindi and English. More Indian languages are planned.
Do I need a new phone number?
No. You forward calls from your existing clinic number to NEPA. We'll guide your team through the setting, and you can switch it off at any time.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. RedClinic runs in the browser on Android phones, iPhones, tablets and computers. There's nothing to install.
How much does it cost?
From Rs {{site.pricing.dayPrice}} a day plus GST, with {{site.pricing.minutes}} call minutes a month, on monthly, six-month or yearly packs. The first {{site.pricing.pilotSeats}} pilot clinics pay Rs {{site.pricing.pilotDayPrice}} a day. See all packs.
Who can see my patients' records?
Only your clinic's staff, with the permissions you give them. Records are kept separate for each clinic. RedClinic's own team can access clinic data to run and support the service, and that access is logged. More on our security page.
