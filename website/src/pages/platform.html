Today: what's happening and what needs you

Open RedClinic in the morning and see three things: today's appointments, what NEPA handled, and the short list of things that need a person.

  • Needs attention collects calls NEPA handed over, unconfirmed appointments, no-shows without a new booking, requests waiting for approval and anything that failed.
  • Urgent items first, then the oldest. Each shows who, what, why and the next step: call back, approve, reschedule or mark done.
  • Nothing disappears. Resolved items leave the list but stay searchable.

Everything the front desk and the doctors need

Patient records

A permanent patient ID that stays the same across visits, even when a phone number changes. Medical history, allergies, notes, visits, calls and documents on one page. Duplicate patients can be found and merged carefully.

Appointments

A daily list and calendar for each doctor. Book, move or cancel, mark arrived, completed or no-show. RedClinic won't let two patients be booked into the same slot.

Documents

Staff upload prescriptions, X-rays, reports and notes as PDF or photos. Each file is linked to the patient, kept in its original form and opened only by people allowed to see it.

Calls

Every call NEPA handles is saved with its transcript, summary and outcome. Corrections by staff are kept separately, so the original stays intact.

Staff and permissions

The clinic owner adds doctors, receptionists and managers, decides what each can see and do, and can switch off an account at once when someone leaves.

Results

Simple counts from your own records: calls answered, appointments NEPA booked, confirmations, no-shows and how many were rebooked. No guesses and no made-up numbers.

Dental workflows, ready on day one

Sign up as a dental clinic and RedClinic sets up a dental pack of follow-up workflows. They're starting templates: your copy is yours to edit, and nothing contacts a patient until you've reviewed it.

Change the number of calls, the timing and the wording, turn workflows off or add your own. Updates we make to the templates never overwrite your changes.
