NEPA, the agent who looks after every patient.

NEPA is your clinic's personal AI agent. It answers calls, brings in new patients, makes the reminder and follow-up calls your team doesn't get to, and writes everything into the RedClinic app. Your staff decide what it's allowed to do.

The work NEPA takes off your desk

NEPA works from the timings, services and answers your clinic approves. It doesn't make things up, and when it doesn't know, it says so and passes the call on.

  • New patients asking for an appointmentTakes their name and reason for visiting, offers free slots and requests the booking.
  • Existing patients booking their next visitRecognises them from their number and checks which doctor they see.
  • Reschedules and cancellationsFinds a new time and sends the change to staff for approval.
  • Questions about the clinicTimings, address, parking, doctors and services, answered the way you wrote them.
  • Confirmation calls before appointmentsCalls patients ahead of their slot, at the time you choose, and records who's coming.
  • Follow-ups after treatmentNext sittings and sessions, review visits, recalls and a check-in after procedures, set up for your speciality.
  • Patients who missed their appointmentCalls to offer a new slot. After two tries it stops and leaves a note for staff.
  • Calls you missedCalls back patients whose calls went unanswered, if you switch this on.

What happens on every call

  1. NEPA introduces itself

    It says it's the clinic's AI assistant. Callers can ask for a person at any point.

  2. It works out who and why

    It matches the caller to their patient record, or creates a new enquiry, and listens for the reason.

  3. It acts within your rules

    It answers from your approved information and checks your real calendar. Bookings wait for staff approval unless you've allowed otherwise.

  4. It writes it all down

    The transcript, a short summary and the outcome go on the patient's record. Anything unfinished lands in Needs attention.

When a call needs a person, NEPA steps aside.

NEPA is a receptionist, not a doctor. It hands the call to your staff, marks it urgent and makes sure nobody loses track of it.

  • Pain, swelling, bleeding or an injury. Transferred at once, never advised.
  • A complaint or an upset patient. Passed to a person with the context so far.
  • A caller who wants a human. Transferred, no questions asked.
  • Anything NEPA isn't sure about. It says it will get the clinic to call back, and adds it to Needs attention.
  • If the transcript fails. The call is marked as untranscribed. NEPA never fills in what it didn't hear.

You set how NEPA talks and what it may do

No prompts or code. Simple settings your clinic owner or manager can change, with a test mode to try changes before patients hear them.

Your clinic's answers

Timings, doctors, services, directions and common questions, in your words. NEPA sticks to them.

Languages and greeting

Choose English, Hindi or Kannada and how NEPA greets callers on your number.

Workflows

Turn each follow-up workflow on or off, and change the number of calls, the timing and what NEPA says.

Permissions

Decide what NEPA may suggest, what needs approval and what it can do automatically.

Test calls

Call NEPA yourself before going live and read back the transcript of each test.

History of changes

Every change to NEPA's settings is saved with who made it, and can be rolled back.

NEPA also helps inside the clinic.

Doctors and staff can ask NEPA, by typing or speaking, to find what's already on file.

  • "When was her last X-ray?" Answered with a link to the original upload.
  • "Who missed appointments this week?" A list, ready for follow-up.
  • "Summarise his last three visits." From your notes, with sources.

NEPA only sees patients the person asking is allowed to see. It doesn't diagnose, prescribe or recommend treatment.

Questions about NEPA

Will NEPA replace my receptionist?
No. NEPA takes the calls your receptionist can't get to and the repetitive follow-ups. Your receptionist still runs the desk, approves bookings and handles anything that needs a person.
Does NEPA sound like a robot?
NEPA uses a natural-sounding voice. The best way to judge is to hear it, which is what the demo is for.
Are calls recorded?
Calls are transcribed and, where permitted, recorded, so your staff can check exactly what was said. Callers are told at the start of the call.
What if a patient asks something NEPA doesn't know?
NEPA says it will ask the clinic to call back, and the question appears in Needs attention with the caller's details. It doesn't guess.
Can NEPA call patients on its own?
Only for workflows your clinic has turned on. Each outgoing call follows the workflow you approved, and patients who withdraw consent are not called.
