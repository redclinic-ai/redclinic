Where skin clinics lose patients

Enquiries come in the evening

People call about acne, pigmentation or hair fall after work, when the front desk has gone home.

Courses stop halfway

A patient books six sessions, misses the fourth and never rebooks. The result suffers and so does word of mouth.

Reviews get forgotten

Long treatments need review visits that nobody remembers to schedule.

After-procedure worries go unheard

A patient with redness after a peel doesn't know whether to call, and the clinic finds out too late.
