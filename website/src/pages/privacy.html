Website privacy notice

This notice covers the redclinic.in website only. How patient data is handled inside the RedClinic product is covered by the terms each clinic accepts, and described on our security page.

Last updated 23 November 2026. Draft pending legal review.

What we collect when you request a demo

  • Your name, mobile number, clinic name and city.
  • How many doctors work at your clinic, and the time you prefer us to call, if you choose one.
  • How you reached the form: the page you were on, the website or campaign that sent you (if any), and whether you used a phone or a computer.
  • Your IP address, kept only to protect the form from spam.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about a RedClinic demo and, if you go ahead, to set up your clinic. We use the source information to understand which of our pages and campaigns help clinics find us.

Who sees it

The RedClinic team. We don't sell your details or share them with advertisers. The service that stores enquiries for us keeps them on our behalf and may not use them for anything else.

Analytics and cookies

This website doesn't set advertising cookies, and it doesn't send your form details to any analytics provider. If we add analytics in future, we'll update this notice and ask for your consent where required.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a customer, we delete your enquiry within 12 months of your last contact with us. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the details you gave us, or to ask us not to call you, email {{site.email}} or call {{site.phoneDisplay}}. We'll respond within 30 days.
